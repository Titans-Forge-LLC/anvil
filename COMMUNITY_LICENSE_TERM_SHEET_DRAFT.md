# ANVIL Community License Term Sheet

Status: `BUSINESS TERMS FROZEN / NOT A LICENSE / LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED`

This term sheet records the intended commercial structure for a future
source-available optimized ANVIL implementation.

- Planned base license: Business Source License 1.1.
- Free-use threshold: less than USD 10,000,000 in consolidated gross revenue
  across the Licensee and its Affiliates during the preceding twelve complete
  calendar months.
- Commercial trigger: USD 10,000,000 or more under the same measurement.
- Transition: ninety calendar days after the threshold is first reached to
  obtain a commercial license or cease Production Use.
- Qualifying production scope: internal operations, customer-facing products,
  SaaS, and managed services.
- Academic grant: limited noncommercial research and teaching regardless of
  organizational revenue.
- Change License: MIT License.
- Change Date: four years after the first public distribution of each version.
- Intended covered material: optimized compiler, codec, and runtime identified
  by an explicit file or repository boundary.
- Separate commercial terms: hosted services, managed services, Forge
  integrations, enterprise governance, support, and negotiated patent rights.
- Open-source carve-out: the public specification, schemas, synthetic examples,
  conformance tests, benchmark harness, basic SDK, and AVP1 reference profile.

Detailed definitions, contribution policy, commercial framing, and the
artifact-license matrix are maintained in `ANVIL_Lab/licensing/`.

The remaining material legal decision is the patent grant or reservation.
Final BSL text must also receive legal review and identify an exact version,
file manifest, first-distribution date, and Change Date before it can take
effect. This term sheet does not license any optimized ANVIL implementation.
